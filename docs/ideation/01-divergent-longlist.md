# Divergent long-list (Ideate, pass 1: unfiltered)

Method: for each track, force at least one idea through each of 6 **paradigm flips** that escape "generic app or chatbot":
- **Adversary**: the AI plays the opponent so you can train against it.
- **Proxy**: the AI acts in the world *on your behalf*.
- **Student**: you teach the AI, and its confusion reveals the gaps in your own understanding.
- **Simulator / builder**: the AI generates a bespoke interactive artifact, not prose.
- **Verifier with tools**: the AI calls real registries, APIs, and sensors and shows evidence.
- **Instrument**: the AI is a creative medium you play, not a vending machine.

Judging is deferred. Research results and the critique pass will filter this list.

| # | Track | Paradigm | Seed idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Cyber | Proxy + Verifier | **AI "stand-in" for risky first contact.** For rental listings, job offers, or marketplace sellers, an AI agent with its own real inbox (Agentboxd) emails the counterparty, asks the verification questions scammers can't satisfy, runs registry/domain/image checks, and hands you a cited trust dossier. You never expose your own email or money until it clears. |
| C2 | Cyber | Adversary | **Scam flight-simulator.** A realistic AI scammer calls or texts you in a safe sandbox (voice-clone "relative", fake bank, fake recruiter). Afterwards it gives a debrief that names each manipulation tactic. Based on psychological inoculation ("prebunking") research. |
| C3 | Cyber | Verifier | **Deepfake call challenge generator.** It issues randomised liveness challenges mid-video-call that real-time face-swaps struggle with, plus a family "safe-word" protocol. |
| C4 | Cyber | Proxy | **Scam ER: the golden-hour responder.** Just been scammed? It triages in 60 seconds, then produces an ordered action list (bank, Action Fraud, credit freeze), a pre-filled evidence pack, and warnings about follow-up "recovery scams". |
| C5 | Cyber | Adversary/Proxy | **"Try to scam our agent" arena.** A public honeypot where people attempt to trick a guarded AI agent. It doubles as a crowd-sourced scam-script dataset. |
| H1 | Health | Student (inverted) | **Teach-back discharge coach.** The patient explains their care plan back, by voice and in their own language. The AI checks that explanation against the clinician's instructions, closes the gaps, and prints a pictogram fridge sheet and a carer summary. |
| H2 | Health | Simulator | **Appointment rehearsal.** A voice interview the night before a 10-min GP slot produces a prioritised 1-page "what I need to say". It also role-plays the GP's likely questions. |

| K4 | Climate | Proxy | **Repair-first agent.** Photograph a broken item. It diagnoses the fault, finds parts and repair cafés, and estimates the money and CO₂ saved versus replacing it. |

## Early instincts (to be tested, not trusted)
- **C1** is the most "paradigm-fresh" and sponsor-aligned option (Agentboxd sponsors the cyber track prize). Judges can test it live by trying to scam it, which also suits Audience Favorite.
- **E1** has the biggest "wow" moment (a bespoke simulation appears and your wrong idea visibly fails). The risk is generating reliable sims in 4 days.
- **H1** has the clearest evidence base (teach-back is an AHRQ-endorsed method) and inverts the chatbot. The risk is a less visual demo.
